REV TORQ V36.4.13 — ACCESS + P&L FIX

1. POS/CASHIER ACCESS
- POS/cashier roles only see POS.
- Pomen/mechanic roles see Job Card + Inventory.
- Admin/Owner retain full ERP navigation.
- Reports, Money Out, Expenses, Staff, Settings and financial export are blocked from non-admin roles.
- switchView now rejects unauthorized views instead of relying only on hidden navigation.
- Removed the old hidden localStorage role-switcher / logo-tap access layer.

2. OVERRIDE MODE
- Override Mode remains Admin/Owner only.
- No hidden gesture is required.

3. PROFIT & LOSS
- Previous calculation was not accounting-correct: it effectively used Sales - Expenses and ignored Cost of Sales.
- P&L now calculates:
  Sales
  - Cost of Sales (COGS)
  = Gross Profit
  - Operating Expenses
  = Net Profit / (Loss)
- Historical COGS uses stock SALE movements and the unit_cost recorded at the time of sale.
- Job-card product sales without a stock SALE movement fall back to job_card_items.unit_cost.
- Service/labour-only invoice lines do not invent a product COGS.
- If a product sale has no recorded cost source, the report warns that COGS is incomplete.

4. DATABASE COMPATIBILITY
- invoices.payment_method is never queried.
- Payment method remains sourced from payments.payment_method.

5. LIVE DATABASE CHECK
- Current August 2026 sample data checked against Supabase schema.
- Current sample: Sales RM315.00, historical/fallback COGS RM129.00, Expenses RM0.00, Gross Profit RM186.00, Net Profit RM186.00.
- Three normal POS invoices use stock SALE unit_cost.
- Job product invoices use job_card_items.unit_cost where stock SALE rows are absent.
